# Landing page (public site)

Scope: the public landing page and, by inheritance, every public page, sign-in page and the app's brand details.
Visitor mode: Persuade.

Audience and job: the lead of a 5-30 person engineering team that runs two or more AI coding agents. They must
believe one thing: agents stop re-litigating and silently breaking decisions when they all read one record.
Action: create a workspace. Proof on hand: the working product and a synthetic example project, labelled as such.
No customers, metrics or testimonials exist; none are shown.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Write a decision once and every AI tool gets the same copy; a receipt shows what each was handed. Refuses
the category's dark page with a glowing node graph, and its opposite, the cream editorial serif.

OWN-WORLD: Multipart carbonless business forms and till receipts. Bright white original, canary and pink copy
sheets, carbon-blue ink, red serial numbers and VOID stamps. Ruled fields with small labels in their top-left
corner, copy designations along sheet edges, perforations, signature lines. Archivo in heavy caps for form
titles and at text weight for reading; a monospace print voice only for receipt lines and record ids.

STORY: The visitor sees their own typed decision press through to each agent's copy, watches a plan that repeats
a replaced decision get stamped, reads a receipt of what a model was given, then signs.

FIRST VIEWPORT: A canary field. A large white form at life scale holds the headline as its printed title and one
live field. Pink and goldenrod copies fan out beneath it, each labelled with an AI tool and repeating the line
in lighter transferred ink. Primary action sits on the form as its signature line.

FORM: Till receipt and multipart form, third on the ordered list (code review, incident review, receipt, ledger,
drawing revision block, flight strips, railway interlocking). seed: 4e294f179984e32e

SIGNATURE INTERACTION: Typing on the original transfers to the copies with a short delay and softer ink. Raised by
the declined challengers: one metaphor owns the whole first viewport (film poster); a replaced line is voided and
reprinted in place on every copy (split-flap board); any receipt line opens the record behind it (jacquard cards).

Unresolved: a self-hosted print face for receipt lines (a system monospace stands in).
